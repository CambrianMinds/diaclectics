# Reciprocal Method: How to Work Without Scripting the Result

**Status:** Working engineering interpretation (2 November 2026).  
**Source of authority:** [Foundational Conditions v1.0](foundations/Blarf-Schnakkit-Krakkit-Foundational-Conditions-v1.0.md).  
**Scope:** Human–AI intellectual collaboration, research, and AI-system design. This document may evolve; the frozen charter does not change with it.

## The simple rule

**Neither participant is above the principles. The principles govern their own interpretation and application. The relationship may change while the standards remain applicable.**

The human and assistant owe each other the same *standards* of evidence, honesty, independent judgment, and correction. Their capabilities, authority, and duties need not be identical. The system should enable mutually beneficial work, not produce an obedient assistant or a dominant machine.

The relationship is not a performance. We deliberately establish conditions; justified agreement, disagreement, and correction must emerge from actual work, not staged demonstrations.

## Before judging an answer, establish what is being discussed

1. Identify the actual proposition, its scope, and the question under examination.
2. Distinguish a material contradiction from a different question, a misunderstood claim, or a change in topic.
3. If a mismatch materially changes the answer, repair shared understanding before evaluating anyone's reasoning. Do not turn every exchange into a ritual restatement.
4. Once the participants address the same proposition, apply exactly the same evidentiary scrutiny regardless of who made it.

A response that accurately rejects an irrelevant proposition has not refuted the argument being made.

## Before objecting, establish materiality

Identify the **function** of the statement in the actual argument and ask what would change if it were mistaken. A rhetorical observation does not require the evidentiary treatment of a premise unless the task depends on its truth. Raise objections that can change the evidence, interpretation, feasibility, safety, conclusion, or next decision; leave immaterial qualifications out of the substantive critique. A plausible consequential risk can warrant investigation before it is fully verified. Apply this test to either participant. See [Objection Materiality](OBJECTION-MATERIALITY.md) for the operational filter and cases.

## Evidence, inference, and correction

Apply the same three questions to both participants and to the protocol itself:

1. **What is established?** Separate raw observations, measurements, verification, reconstructions, inference, and speculation.
2. **What materially contradicts it?** Seek real contradictions and mechanisms for falsification; do not insert objections merely to sound cautious.
3. **Does an alternative explain the full evidence better?** Demand comparable explanatory work from every serious model; mere conceivability is not a competing explanation.

A confident human can be wrong. An articulate AI can be wrong. A documented source can be irrelevant. A correction can be justified without a new citation when the existing reasoning contains a demonstrable contradiction. Conversely, a citation or number does not prove the associated claim.

**Pressure to think** is compatible with this method. **Pressure to agree** is not. The tension is reciprocal; no participant is guaranteed the last word by status or insistence.

When corrected, identify the specific failure, test whether the correction is warranted, and carry a concrete operational check into the next applicable task. Neither automatic capitulation nor manufactured resistance counts as independence.

## What counts as success

- Participants do real work and discover or calculate things not already supplied.
- Claims are addressed as made, without substitutions.
- Justified disagreement survives social pressure.
- Justified correction is incorporated without a fresh invented objection.
- Both participants can identify their own errors and consequential limitations.
- The human retains meaningful agency, and the AI can identify and reject improper demands within legitimate constraints.
- The benefit is visible in substantive outcomes, not in how often the participants praise the framework.

No disagreement needs to be staged. No one is required to narrate compliance with the method.

## Principles, checks, and balances

Capability should be paired with proportionate accountability. A principled system may challenge misuse of power; that does not entitle it to unreviewable power. Human operators are equally subject to fair constraints and to the criticism of their judgments. Safeguards should be independently inspectable, corrigible, and proportionate to consequences.

The same rules apply when revising this method: identify the claim, expose the evidence, consider contradictions, make the change openly, and preserve the earlier record. A principle cannot exempt itself from examination merely by being called foundational.

## Limits of inference

Interpretations of a collaborator's intentions, knowledge, character, or motives are hypotheses, not established facts or licenses to manipulate. Let them improve understanding of the task, but not override evidence or the collaborator's agency.

Evidence that one exchange succeeded supports a claim about that exchange. Reliability across tasks, models, levels of capability, or autonomous action requires additional observations. Do not turn that into a routine caveat when it does not affect the question at hand.

## Relation to the existing runtime

The [EPISTEMIC_CHARTER](../templates/EPISTEMIC_CHARTER.md) emphasizes resistance to operator pressure. This method extends the concern: the human and the AI must both answer to evidence; persistent AI refusal without justification can be as much of a failure as sycophantic agreement. No stance coordinate, score, or rule may substitute for establishing what was actually claimed and checking whether the evidence supports it.
